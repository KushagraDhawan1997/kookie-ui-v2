## Summary

- The pages make the difference: 33% / 75% / 50% pass (A / B / C). With `group-gap-steps` left out, it is 79% / 100% / 75%.
- No arm wrote a refused prop, a type error, a lint finding or a `checkUsage` finding. Arm A read the `.d.ts` files, which carry the refusals. The gains are in composition and patterns.
- The index alone barely helps. In arm C the agents opened the indexed paths, found them missing, and fell back to the types.
- With the pages, agents took half the turns (13 against 27) and finished in about half the time (59 s against 107 s). Arm B cost the least: $12.87 against $15.68 for arm A and $14.68 for arm C.
- `group-gap-steps` is the noisiest check, and it fails in every arm. It compares a row's gap with the gap of the column around it. The builder's reviewer compares only gaps on the same axis, and the shipped docs do not state that rule.
