Kookie groups its components by the job a person is doing. Start with the job, and you have a
short list of components to choose from.

## The jobs

- **Act:** Make something happen. `Button`, `Menu`, `Toolbar`, `Command`.
- **Choose:** Pick from options. `Checkbox`, `Switch`, `RadioGroup`, `SegmentedControl`,
  `Select`, `Combobox`, `Slider`.
- **Enter:** Type a value. `TextField`, `TextArea`, `NumberField`, `Field`.
- **Go:** Move to another place. `Shell`, `Page`, `Tabs`, `NavTree`, `Breadcrumb`, `Link`.
- **Browse:** Find one item in a collection. `Table`, `Tree`, `Row`, `Carousel`.
- **Read:** Take in content. `Text`, `Heading`, `Card`, `CodeBlock`, `Accordion`.
- **Know:** Learn the state of something. `Notice`, `Badge`, `Chip`, `Progress`, `Tooltip`.
- **Focus:** Deal with one thing first. `Popover`, `Dialog`, `Sheet`, `AlertDialog`.
- **Converse:** Talk back and forth. `Composer`, `MessageScroller`.
- **Arrange:** Place things on the page. `Box`, `Flex`, `Stack`, `Grid`, `Surface`.

Arrange is your job as the builder, not the person's. The [components](/components) index
lists every component under its job.

## Components and patterns

A component does one thing. A pattern coordinates several parts and makes the decisions its job
always needs. `Shell`, `Composer` and `Field` are patterns, and the index marks each one.

## Components that look alike

Two components can look the same and do different jobs. Every component page has a "Use
instead" section that names its look-alike and says when to use each.

- **Switch or Checkbox:** A switch takes effect at once. A checkbox waits for a submit.
- **Tabs or SegmentedControl:** Tabs change the view. A segmented control sets a value.
- **Menu or Select:** A menu runs an action. A select keeps the value you picked.
- **Popover or Dialog:** A popover leaves the page usable. A dialog needs full attention.
- **Link or Button:** A link goes somewhere. A button does something here.
